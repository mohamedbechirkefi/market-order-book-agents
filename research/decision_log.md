## D-001 (2026-11-24): custom non-commercial licence instead of a permissive code licence

- **Decided by:** the owner.
- **Decision:** all original material in this repository is under the MARKET Source-Available Non-Commercial Research License v1.0 (SPDX `LicenseRef-MARKET-NC-Research-1.0`), with a CLA (`CLA.md`) and the files `LICENSE`, `NOTICE.md`, `LICENSE_HISTORY.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `.github/CODEOWNERS`, `.github/workflows/cla.yml`.
- **Rationale:** the owner's own protection. The master prompt (section 2) asks for an "owner-approved permissive code license"; the owner chose this protective licence instead, which is the owner's right as the authority over licensing.
- **Alternatives:** MIT, Apache-2.0 or another permissive licence, as the master prompt suggests.
- **Consequences:** the repository is source-available, not OSI open-source. README, CITATION.cff and any article must not call it "open source". Third-party data and dependencies keep their own licences (`data/manifest.json`, `uv.lock`). Licence files are not modified by sessions.
